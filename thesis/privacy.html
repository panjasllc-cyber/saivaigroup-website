What we collect

If you choose to sign up for emails, we collect your email address, your first name if you give it, the topics you ticked, and the date and time you signed up. Signing up is optional; the resources are free without it. The hub sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising code of its own.

How we use and store it

We use your sign-up information only to send the kinds of emails you ticked. It is stored in a private database in Notion, a service of Notion Labs, Inc., which processes it for us; it is sent there by a small sign-up function that runs on Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc. We do not sell or share personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We keep sign-up records until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and delete inactive records after 24 months; after you unsubscribe we keep only your email address on a do-not-email list so we don’t contact you again.

Our web hosts

The hub’s pages are served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. If you sign up, your submission goes to a small function on Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., which checks it and stores it in Notion. GitHub and Cloudflare may each keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect their services, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We may be able to see some of that technical information through our accounts with them, and we use it only to run and protect the hub. Our sign-up function uses your IP address only for a moment, to limit repeated submissions; it does not store it.

If you write to us

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we do not add you to any list. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete your correspondence, by writing to arussell@saivaigroup.com. We respond within 30 days. California residents have the rights described in the California Consumer Privacy Act, to the extent it applies to us; contact us the same way to exercise them. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email or by writing to arussell@saivaigroup.com.

Because we don’t track visitors across sites, we don’t change anything in response to a browser Do Not Track signal.
